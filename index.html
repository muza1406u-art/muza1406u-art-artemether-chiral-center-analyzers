About this Model

Artemether is a derivative of artemisinin and contains multiple chiral centers. This simplified educational model focuses on one tetrahedral stereocenter and demonstrates the Cahn–Ingold–Prelog priority method.

  1. Assign substituent priorities (1 highest to 4 lowest).
  2. Orient priority 4 away from the viewer.
  3. Trace 1 → 2 → 3: clockwise = R, counterclockwise = S.
